Service areas

Coating floors across Kingman and northern Mohave County.

Our crews work throughout the Kingman area and surrounding communities. If you are not sure whether we reach your property, just ask.

Kingman

Most of our work is right here in town, from older homes near downtown to newer neighborhoods along Stockton Hill Road and the north side. Kingman garages range from tidy single-car spaces to oversized bays built for trucks and toys, and many commercial floors sit along Andy Devine Avenue, Beverly Avenue and the industrial areas near the airport.

Butler

Just outside city limits, Butler properties tend to have bigger lots, detached workshops and RV garages. Those slabs are often large and sometimes older, so moisture testing and a heavier-duty system are common recommendations. Many owners pair a coated shop floor with a separate flake system in the attached garage.

Golden Valley

Golden Valley’s open parcels and long driveways bring a lot of wind-blown dust into garages and shops. Textured, mid-tone flake floors are popular here because they hide grit and stay grippy. We also coat a fair number of steel-building slabs where flexible polyurea base coats make sense.

Valle Vista

Homes around the Valle Vista golf course often have extended garages for golf carts as well as vehicles. Clean, light flake blends and polyaspartic topcoats keep those floors bright and resistant to tire marks, and covered patios are frequent add-on projects.

Hualapai Mountain area

At higher elevation, cooler nights and occasional snow mean freeze-thaw cycles matter more for patios, entries and garage aprons. We pay extra attention to drainage, joints and cure temperatures on projects up the mountain.

Chloride

North along US 93, Chloride has historic homes, shops and outbuildings with character — and slabs with a long history. Older concrete may carry old paint or sealers that need to be ground off before a new system can bond.

Dolan Springs

Properties around Dolan Springs often combine a home with a detached garage or workshop. We coordinate travel and scheduling so larger projects can be completed efficiently.

Hackberry & Valentine

Along old Route 66, ranch properties often have equipment sheds, barns and shop floors that see heavy use. Sealers and dust-proofing are sometimes a better fit than full coatings for these spaces, and we will help you choose.

Outside these communities?

We occasionally take on projects farther afield, especially larger garages, shops and commercial floors where the travel is worthwhile. Send us the location and a short description of the project. We will tell you plainly whether we can schedule it and what that might look like.

Every service we offer is available throughout our area, including garage floor coatings, decorative flake floors, patio coatings and commercial flooring.
