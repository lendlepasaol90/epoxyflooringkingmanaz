About us

Concrete coating, done the slow way where it counts.

We are a Kingman-area flooring crew focused on one thing: coated concrete floors that stay bonded and look right for years.

How we think about floors

Every coated floor is a stack of decisions. How deep to grind. Whether a crack is stable or still moving. Which primer to use on a slab that tested a little damp. How thick to build the base so it survives a floor jack. None of those decisions are visible once the topcoat goes on, which is exactly why they are easy to skip and why we take our time with them.

Our customers range from homeowners with a single-car garage to shop owners and facility managers with thousands of square feet. The scale changes, but the approach does not. We look at the concrete first, explain what we see, and recommend a system that fits the way the space is used — not just the system that is easiest to sell.

What you can expect from us

  • A real on-site visit before any price is given, including moisture and adhesion checks where needed.
  • A written scope that lists the prep, repairs and every coating layer, so you can compare quotes fairly.
  • Diamond grinding with dust collection instead of acid etching.
  • Honest advice when a different product — or no coating at all — is the better answer.
  • A clean job site, a clear schedule and straightforward care instructions when we leave.

Built around high-desert conditions

Working in and around Kingman has shaped how we choose materials. Intense sun means UV-stable topcoats on anything that sees daylight. Temperature swings mean paying attention to joints and flexible base coats in uninsulated buildings. Wind and dust mean colors and textures that look clean between sweeps. These are not theoretical concerns; they are the reasons coatings fail here, and planning for them is most of the job.

If you want to understand the options before talking to anyone, the service pages are a good place to start. Each one covers how the system works, where it fits, and what to ask any installer you are considering — including us.
