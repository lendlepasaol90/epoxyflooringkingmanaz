Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Epoxy Flooring Kingman AZ handles information submitted through this website.

Information we collect

When you submit an estimate request, we collect the details you provide: your name, phone number, email address, project type and any message. We also receive basic technical information such as the page you submitted from.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your request, schedule an estimate and communicate about your project. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Third-party services

This site uses Google Maps embeds and Google Fonts, which may collect usage data under Google’s own privacy policy. Form submissions are processed through our hosting provider.

Contact

To ask about or remove your information, call (928) 000-0000 or use our contact page.
